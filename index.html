How It Works

From Enquiry to Disbursement

  1. 1

    Submit Enquiry

    Share your project and funding requirement through the form below or a call with your relationship manager.

  2. 2

    Initial Screening

    We review project stage, ticket size and security to identify a realistic funding path.

  3. 3

    Document Collection

    You receive a checklist by category — applicant, financial, property and project documents.

  4. 4

    Credit Assessment

    Financials, DSCR, promoter contribution and project economics are evaluated.

  5. 5

    Legal Verification

    Title, encumbrance and approvals are examined by our legal desk.

  6. 6

    Technical Assessment

    Site valuation, construction stage and cost-to-complete are assessed.

  7. 7

    Lender Approval

    The file is submitted to the matched lender for credit decisioning.

  8. 8

    Sanction

    Terms, tenure, pricing and conditions are issued in the sanction letter.

  9. 9

    Documentation

    Loan agreement and security documentation are executed.

  10. 10

    Disbursement

    Funds are released, typically tranche-wise against construction milestones.

Required Documents

What to Keep Ready

Applicant & KYC Documents
  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar / identity proof
  • Address proof
  • GST registration
  • Company incorporation documents, partnership deed, MOA/AOA (as applicable)
  • Board resolution authorising the borrowing
Financial Documents
  • Income Tax Returns (last 3 years)
  • Audited balance sheet & P&L
  • Bank statements (last 12 months)
  • GST returns
  • Existing loan statements
  • CMA data / financial projections
Property Documents
  • Title deed / sale deed
  • Property card / 7-12 extract
  • Mutation records
  • Search & encumbrance report
Project Documents
  • Approved building plans
  • Commencement certificate
  • RERA registration
  • Architect's cost certificate
  • Construction schedule & sales report

FAQ

Common Questions

What is construction finance?

Construction finance is funding provided to builders, developers and contractors to construct a residential, commercial or mixed-use project, typically released in tranches as construction progresses.

How much funding can I get?

It depends on project cost, security, promoter contribution, project stage and lender policy. Use the eligibility calculator above for an indicative range, or apply for a proper assessment.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary by lender and case complexity, but a well-documented file moves faster through credit, legal and technical stages. Your relationship manager will give you a realistic estimate for your specific case.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Final approval, pricing, terms, security and disbursement are always subject to the lender's policy, eligibility criteria, documentation and due diligence.

Do you charge for the eligibility check?

No, the initial eligibility discussion and calculator are free.

Can you help if my project is mid-construction?

Yes — construction completion funding and balance transfer/refinance are common requests and part of our regular solution set.
