Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Solitaire Finz Mart ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, use the eligibility calculator, or request a callback, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, mobile number, email, company name, project details and funding requirement.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and assess your funding requirement.
  • To route your enquiry to the relevant relationship manager or team.
  • To contact you regarding your application, by phone, email or WhatsApp.
  • To maintain internal records for compliance and service quality.

How We Store Your Information

Enquiry data is stored in a secured cloud database with access restricted to authorised staff roles. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing With Lenders

If you proceed with a formal application, relevant information is shared with the bank(s) or NBFC(s) you choose to apply through, as required for credit assessment and processing.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to ask that we stop contacting you.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@solitairefinzmart.example.
