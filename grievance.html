Support

Grievance / Support

If you have a concern about your application, an interaction with our team, or any aspect of our service, we want to hear about it.

How to Reach Us

  • Phone: +91 00000 00000
  • Email: contact@solitairefinzmart.example
  • WhatsApp: Chat with us

What to Include

Please share your name, mobile number used at enquiry/application, application or enquiry reference (if any), and a brief description of the issue. This helps us resolve it faster.

Resolution Timeline

We aim to acknowledge grievances within 2 working days and provide a resolution or update within 7 working days. Complex matters involving a lender's internal process may take longer, and we will keep you informed of the status.

Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the initial response, please ask to have your concern escalated to the Managing Partner.
